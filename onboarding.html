ABOUT CSPC S.A.F.E.

S.A.F.E. (Suggestions And Feedback Engagement) is a student voice and accountability portal for Camarines Sur Polytechnic Colleges. It gives the campus community a place to share suggestions, submit feedback, and follow the progress of campus concerns.

Students can propose improvements, report issues, and support ideas through community upvoting. Clear updates help make campus feedback more visible and actionable.

OUR MISSION

Our mission is to make student feedback accessible, transparent, and constructive by connecting the CSPC community with the people working to improve campus life.
